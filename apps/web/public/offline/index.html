Keep a dictionary on this phone

  1. In your workspace, open Offline phone and export an approved dictionary pack. Save it to Files.
  2. On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the installed app while online.
  3. Import your pack below. Wait for “Ready for offline use”, then try airplane mode.

Import saves a private copy on this device and replaces the previous pack. Keep your export in Files as a backup; clearing website data removes the saved dictionary. Workspace sign-out does not remove this copy.

Exact matches ignore case and repeated spaces. Spelling and phrase suggestions need your review. This matcher cannot infer new translations or understand paraphrases with no shared wording.
